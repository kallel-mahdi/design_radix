Attention Mechanisms in Neural Network Architectures for Scientific Literature Mining

Sarah Chen¹*, Marcus Rivera², Elena Kowalski¹, James Thompson³

Abstract: We present a comprehensive analysis of transformer-based attention mechanisms for extracting structured knowledge from scientific papers. Our approach combines multi-head self-attention with domain-specific pretraining, achieving state-of-the-art results on citation extraction and entity recognition benchmarks. We demonstrate that academic-focused language models significantly outperform general-purpose alternatives in specialized scientific tasks.

1. Introduction

The exponential growth of scientific literature presents unprecedented challenges for knowledge synthesis and discovery [1]. Researchers increasingly rely on computational approaches to navigate the vast corpus of academic publications [2, 3]. Traditional keyword-based retrieval methods prove inadequate for capturing the nuanced semantic relationships inherent in scholarly discourse.
